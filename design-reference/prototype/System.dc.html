06 · Icons & illustration

Drawn like the logo.

  • 2.6px ink stroke, round caps, Bubblegum fill accents.
  • The four-point sparkle and the soap bubble come straight from the logo art. Use two or three per view, not twenty.
  • The supplied logo and Linda's badge are used as-is. They are never redrawn, recolored or cropped through the artwork.
07 · Motion

Something always gets cleaner.

The Clean SweepHero load: a mop wipes the dusty page clean. 1.7s, once, replayable.
The Boss TreatmentCTA pulls toward the cursor (max 12px), gloss sweep, three-sparkle pop.
The Transformation RevealDrag, tap or arrow-key the mop handle across the room.
The Sweep TrackQuote progress mops itself from dusty to pink, 0.6s per step.
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) reveals · (.2,.9,.3,1.5) presses
  • Motion shows cause and effect: wiped, lifted or revealed. Nothing moves just to move.
  • One hero moment per screen.
  • Reduced motion: every animation is removed, the page loads already clean, and every control still works.
08 · Photography

Real homes, real results.

  • Bright daylight, true color, no heavy filters. Clean should look clean, not retouched.
  • Before and after pairs shot from the same spot, same lens, same time of day.
  • Details over wide shots: the faucet, the grout line, the folded towel.
  • No stock photos and no generated images presented as customer work. Until real photos exist, the dashed gold placeholder stays.
